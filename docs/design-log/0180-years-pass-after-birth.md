# 0180: Births end with "some years later"

- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Every birth cutscene ends with a page of narration in the
  book's voice: childhood passed over in a few lines, then "Some years
  pass. You are grown." Play starts with you grown. Below (an irregular
  raised in the Undercroft) it is chalk on slate, and the Gleaner's
  greeting is to the grown-up.
