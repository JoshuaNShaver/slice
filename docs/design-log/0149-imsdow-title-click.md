# 0149: imsdow, the architect; click her for a new line

- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Her name tag reads "imsdow — the architect" (`title` in
  `imsdow.cfg`). Clicking or tapping her (a pointing-hand cursor) rolls a
  new line from her puns, lore and reflections, never the one just said;
  1 in 6 clicks makes her blush instead (with a `blushing` line). Asleep,
  a click is a nudge: it counts like a command toward waking her.
- **Why:** the user asked for a title and for clicking her to roll new text.
