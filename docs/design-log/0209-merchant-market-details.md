# 0209: Merchant market details

- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Status:** Proposed (built)
- **Decision:** How log 0205's markets work, as built:
  - The Harbourmaster (dock) sells logs from one stock shared by everyone,
    for chits (210-420, 260 normal) or coins (12-50, 28 normal). The price
    follows the stock. Logs workmen sell go into it; a crew adds one every
    30 s up to 40; every few minutes a glut or a shortage swings it by
    10-20.
  - Work orders: up to 10 logs at 1.5x the dock's coin price, paid up
    front. Workmen's deliveries fill them first, the crew one every 20 s
    otherwise; the logs wait at the dock (saved, up to 30) to collect.
  - Merchants' Row is on the Center's east rise; the Market Clerk shows
    your booth's orders: up to 3 at once, a new one every 90 s, 2-8 logs at
    about 19-48 coins each, open for 10 minutes. Not saved.
