# 0067: Triangles serve in the army; a campaign ends in retirement

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Triangles fight through the barracks (log 0061), on missions
  against the Colour Revolt (log 0068). Missions give only XP and
  combatant pay (log 0071). A triangle earns a marriage certificate by
  completing a campaign: working through the Revolt far enough to defeat
  one of its bosses (an upper-class polygon of the Revolt). The certificate
  is a retirement of sorts.
- **Why:** the book's isosceles are soldiers; the cave is now closed to them.
