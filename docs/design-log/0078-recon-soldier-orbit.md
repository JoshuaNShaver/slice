# 0078: Recon soldiers orbit you

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** On recon, the soldier with nothing to fight stays close by
  orbiting: it rolls past you to a spot about 130 px on your other side
  and waits; once you've moved about 70 px, it rolls past you the other
  way and waits again. Invasion soldiers still just follow.
- **Why:** a lone companion should feel like it's moving with you, not
  trailing behind.
