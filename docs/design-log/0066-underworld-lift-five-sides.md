# 0066: The underworld lift takes pentagons and up

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Guards on the underworld lift admit only regular shapes with
  5+ sides. Triangles and squares can no longer reach the Undercroft or the
  training cave. Irregulars still can never ride up.
- **Why:** triangles are relegated to the military (log 0067).
- **Replaces** log 0058.
