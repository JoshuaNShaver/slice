# 0183: Quests belong to the player, not the family

- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** A quest is work critical to the game's plot, and its
  progress is the player's (the account's): it outlives families, so a new
  family carries on where the last one left off, and a finished quest
  stays finished. Progress that belongs to one family (tasks, missions)
  is a separate thing, kept with the family. Quest characters never act
  as if a new family were the same person they knew.
  Supersedes the first point of log 0182.
