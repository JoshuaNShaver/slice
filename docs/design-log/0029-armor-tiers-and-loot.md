# 0029: Armor tiers and per-player loot

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Status:** Accepted (armor-only gear and auto-equip superseded by 0036; plating look by 0069)
- **Decision:** Each cave room drops the next tier of armor: rose (tier 1),
  mint (2), lilac (3), and the Pretender gilded (4). The boss always drops a
  marriage certificate.
  *Proposed:* armor gives rigidity 4 / 9 / 15 / 22. Room enemies drop
  armor 15% of the time (boosted by luck). Loot is per player, like Realm of
  the Mad God: each player who hit the enemy gets their own roll, and only
  they can pick it up. Better armor is worn as soon as you touch it, and
  worse armor stays on the ground. Unclaimed items vanish after 90 s. Armor
  shows as a colored inner band, so the outline never changes.
