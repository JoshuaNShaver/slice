# 0059: The underworld has its own hub

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** The underworld has a hub of its own (the Undercroft) with
  everything the Center has: a respawn point, a healer and a priest who
  marries irregulars. Irregulars spawn there, and the home key takes them
  there. Its houses break the book's pentagon law: crooked, lopsided,
  even triangles and squares.
- **Why:** irregulars can't leave, so the underworld must be a full game.
