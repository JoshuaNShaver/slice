# 0215: Stretches of sea, rain, and jumping waves

- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Status:** Accepted (numbers *proposed*); the rain's swell superseded by 0218, wave jumps by 0219
- **Decision:** Each stretch of open sea has its own obstacles, rougher the
  further west. The Sound (dock to the Logging Isle) stays as it is. The
  Narrows (Logging Isle to the Quarry Isle): every 128 s spell of weather
  has a 50% chance of rain, which comes on over 15 s and makes the waves
  35% taller while it lasts. Weather comes from the server's clock, so
  every peer sees the same. Small boats (the one we have) can jump waves
  like a bobber: run fast down one wave into the next, the hull is driven
  deeper than it floats, and as it rises it's thrown clear, riders and
  all; deeper and faster throws higher (up to ~420 px/s up).
- **Why:** each crossing should feel different; the jump rewards speed in
  rough water.
