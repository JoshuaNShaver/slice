# 0056: Chalk on slate for the underworld

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Decision:** The underworld inverts the parchment look rather than the
  colors: a dark slate ground with pale chalk outlines, keeping the pastel
  fills. The change fades in over the hatch shaft, not at a hard cut.
- **Why:** a literal color inversion turns pastels into muddy darks and
  makes the HUD and gear tints harder to learn; swapping only ground and
  ink keeps them readable.
