# 0082: Speech bubbles, thought clouds, parchment tooltips

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** An NPC's line shows in a parchment bubble above them, with a
  dashed line down to the speaker. Lines with no speaker nearby (narration,
  the General from the front) and your own news (level up, pickups,
  marriage) show in a thought cloud beside you, trailing dots back to you.
  Up to 3 lines stack in a bubble. Place names stay as a top banner.
  Tooltips are see-through parchment, offset a little more from the cursor.
- **Why:** text sits near whoever it belongs to, instead of at the very top.
