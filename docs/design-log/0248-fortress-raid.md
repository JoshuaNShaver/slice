# 0248: The rangers' mission: a fortress raid by sea

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Status:** Accepted (route, boats, capsizing, boss); Proposed (numbers, wall foes)
- **Decision:** A rangers-only mission from the General, up to 4
  players. A boatman at the start spawns a slightly bigger boat (two
  pontoons, or a rowboat) that he rows once spoken to; all players can
  ride it, or each spawn their own. You cross the sea in a storm to an
  enemy fortress, leave the boat, climb the walls, cross the top of a
  wall and let down the gate at its end. An army troop charges in and
  you fight the fortress commander, a boss. Capsizing ends the mission
  for that player.
  - *Proposed:* recommended level 20; painted skirmishers and melee
    rebels hold the wall top; pay 250 chits; the boss carries the usual
    1-in-5 discharge with a marriage certificate (log 0092).
