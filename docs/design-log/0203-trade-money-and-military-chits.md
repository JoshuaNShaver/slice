- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Status:** Superseded by 0204

  only from military work and spent at the barracks. *Proposed name:*
  **chits** (army paper only the Quartermaster honours). Goods like logs

  **electrum** (the pale gold-silver alloy of the first coins). Neither
  shows on the HUD: both move to a Purse tab in the Tab panel. Supersedes
  log 0071's name for pay and log 0026's top-right currency corner.
