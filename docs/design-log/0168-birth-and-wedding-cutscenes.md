# 0168: Birth and wedding cutscenes

- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** A cutscene plays at every birth (a new family's first
  triangle, and each heir). A priest speaks over the newborn: its
  measurements and regularity, its likely occupation, and joy or dread to
  match, all following the book. An irregular newborn is sent away and
  wakes in the Undercroft, talking to someone there. A second cutscene
  plays at a wedding: the priest marries you and congratulates you. This
  needs female characters (brides). Staging, lines and who the brides are
  are still open (§9).
