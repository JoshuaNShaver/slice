# 0219: Ribbons trail behind; boats no longer jump waves

- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Status:** Accepted; the ribbon part superseded by 0220
- **Decision:** Regimental ribbons trail behind the shape as it moves (fully behind it from 120 px/s, easing round as it turns), and stream west on a light wind only at rest. The boat's wave jump (the bobber, log 0215) is removed: boats ride the waves and no longer throw themselves clear.
- **Why:** ribbons that ignored movement looked wrong; the jump felt clunky.
