# 0254: Rangers wear camouflage

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Status:** Accepted (pigment for rangers, real stealth, five-slot ranger sets, muted dappled patterns); Proposed (numbers)
- **Decision:** Ranger tiers have a pigment piece, **camouflage**, sold by
  the ranger quartermaster like the rest (Scout for chits). A full ranger
  set needs all five slots; army sets still need four and have no pigment.
  Camouflage gives the slot's vigor and hides you: enemies see you from
  closer, until 3 s after you strike or are struck. Each tier is drawn as
  a muted ground colour dappled with a darker one: colour, but quiet. An
  exception to log 0069, as the rangers officially don't exist.
  - *Proposed:* sight cut by Scout 10%, Tracker 18%, Outrider 25%,
    Pathfinder 32%, Ranger 40%; even enemies that see their whole room
    only see a hidden ranger within ~520-620 px times that.
