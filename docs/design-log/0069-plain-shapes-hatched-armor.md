# 0069: Shapes are plain; armor is crosshatching

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Unarmored shapes are plain: ink outline, uncoloured fill.
  Overworld armor is plain too, told apart by crosshatch patterns. Colour
  appears only in the underworld (coming back a little, contraband) and on
  the Colour Revolt.
- **Replaces** the "colored inner band" look of plating in logs 0029 and 0036.
