# 0020: Tumbly momentum rolling and bounce jumps

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Status:** Accepted (exact numbers: proposed tuning)
- **Decision:** Movement should feel bouncy and gymnastic. Holding a roll key
  builds speed over a few seconds, and rolling downhill can carry you past
  that top speed. Shapes are slightly bouncy and keep their spin. Jumping
  again just as you land is a **bounce**: off a flat face it goes higher than
  a normal jump (×1.3 speed), and off a corner it goes higher still (×1.55)
  and flips you with a burst of spin. Jumping works from any resting pose.
- **Replaces:** the "pivot over the leading corner" rolling in 0019, which
  dug corners into the ground and swallowed jumps.
