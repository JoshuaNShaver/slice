# 0028: XP and the level curve

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Decision:** Kills give XP to every player who hit the enemy. Level n → n+1
  costs 20 + 15 × (n − 1) XP, about 2,950 XP for level 20 in all. Bandit 10,
  brute 40, zealot 80, Pretender 400. Aimed at about 30 minutes to level 20
  in the cave (log 0007).
