# 0120: The Online sign-in screen

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Decision:** Choosing Online connects at once and shows one centred
  card for each step: reaching the server (Cancel); can't reach it (Try
  again); "Sign in to play online" with a Google-styled "Sign in with
  Google" button (white, grey outline, the four-colour G); "Finish in your
  browser" (Cancel). Signed in, a "Signed in as … · Sign out" line sits
  above the 3 family slots. Sign out forgets the saved session and
  reconnects, so the sign-in card shows again.
- **Why:** the old flow (Join, Connect, then a plain button in a slot
  card) hid what was happening and looked unfinished.
