# 0194: Flatland's fog as crosshatching, no boil

- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Decision:** The ink pass (log 0191) drops the boiling lines (they
  shimmered like a heatwave) and the darkened edges. In their place,
  Flatland's fog: the farther from your shape on screen, the paler the
  world and the more it's hatched over in quick, broken pen strokes, one
  set and then a crossing set, pinned to the world. Underground the
  strokes are chalk. Ink bleed and paper grain stay. The main menu's
  picture keeps its own fog, so it gets no hatching. Off on Low graphics.
- **Why:** in the book, fog is how Flatlanders judge distance (far things
  are dimmer); hatching shows that depth in ink.
