# 0080: Soldiers jump to get around

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Soldiers on the move jump periodically like a player
  pressing Space: up, forward and tumbling, then steering in the air.
  The further behind they are, the sooner and further they jump. Close
  to a foe they use their usual fighting hops.
- **Why:** rolling alone, they flopped over and fell behind.
