# 0202: The logging island

- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Status:** Accepted (not built)
- **Decision:** The first island has 5–7 large trees in a row, taller
  than the screen. Players hurt a tree by jumping into it. Each tree has
  10,000 health (hidden), and every 1,000 damage drops a log for a random
  player who has hit it. Each log takes one bag slot (no stacking), so a
  full bag is 10 logs. Players sail home to sell them.
- *Proposed:* damage goes through the usual corner hits (force and speed
  count); the log goes to a random hitter weighted by their share of the
  damage; a felled tree regrows after a few minutes.
