# 0250: The fortress raid, as built

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Status:** Accepted (gate, boatman, capsizing); Proposed (the rest); the storm's waves superseded by 0253
- **Decision:** Building log 0248.
  - *Accepted (the user):* the drawbridge comes down when a player uses
    the **winch** at the end of the wall-walk. The **boatman rows as soon
    as anyone speaks to his boat**; others get a rowboat of their own from
    the Boatman on the dock while his is out, or wait for it to come back.
    **Falling into the raid's sea costs the bag, as on the coast, and the
    mission:** the army fishes you out to the barracks, with no pay.
  - *Proposed:* **other seas.** `Ocean` keeps a list of seas besides the
    western one (`add_sea`), each with its own level, seabed, shores and
    weather. A Fortress adds a stormy one (steady rain, waves ×1.7) while
    it exists, on every peer. Boats and drowning work there unchanged.
  - *Proposed:* **rowboats** are 130 px half-width with no sail. The
    boatman is drawn at the stern and rows ~20 s across, holds at the
    landing while anyone's aboard, and rows home after 5 s empty.
  - *Proposed:* **the climb** is six crags rising 120 px each: a bounce
    each, not a plain jump. Two painted rebels and two skirmishers
    (strength 2.5) each keep to their stretch of the wall-walk.
  - *Proposed:* **the Castellan.** Carminus, the Castellan, an irregular
    heptagon (3,800 health, gear tier 4, the Revolt's moves). He comes out
    of his keep with two skirmishers when the bridge drops, and 8 soldiers
    charge in, hurrying (role "charge"). His fall wins it: 250 chits each,
    and the usual discharge roll.
  - *Proposed:* the General lists the raid for everyone, marked "Rangers
    only", and turns away anyone not through ranger school.
