# 0177: Triangle jobs by apex: soldier, workman, trader

- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Status:** Accepted (not built)
- **Decision:** What a triangle may do follows its apex, and each step
  keeps the ones below it:
  - **40°:** soldier only (the drill yard, missions). One pure soldier life.
  - **45° and up:** workman: woodcutting and mining, for goods to sell.
  - **55° and up:** a trading booth.
  - **60° (equilateral):** a tradesman of the middle class, as before.
  Later, goods prices rise, so a workman's earnings speed up soldiering
  (buying gear, provisions and drills). Replaces log 0074.
- **Why:** the book's soldiers are the sharpest triangles and its workmen
  the next; work should feed the war, not replace it.
