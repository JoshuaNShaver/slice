# 0218: Rain doubles the Narrows' waves

- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Status:** Accepted (number *proposed*)
- **Decision:** While it rains on the Narrows its waves are twice as tall (`swell` 2.0, was 1.35 in 0215): the biggest crests reach about 68 px, against an 80 px hull.
- **Why:** 35% taller didn't feel like a storm; the second crossing should feel rough in rain.
