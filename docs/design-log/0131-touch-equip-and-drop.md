# 0131: Double tap to use, drag to the drop zone to drop

- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** On a touch screen, double tapping an item does what a
  double-click does: wear gear from the bag, take worn gear off, drink,
  eat, take from a chest. Two presses on a slot within 0.4 s count as a
  double-click everywhere, since taps don't reliably report one. While the
  bag is open on touch, a drop zone (an arrow onto the ground) sits to its
  left; dragging a bag item onto it drops the item, as right-click does.
- **Why:** touch has no right-click, and double taps weren't registering.
  Resolves 0122's open question on dropping items by touch.
