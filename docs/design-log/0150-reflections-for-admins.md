# 0150: Other admins hear kind words, and plain facts about Josh

- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** In `imsdow_about_josh.cfg` (admins other than Josh), about
  two thirds of imsdow's `reflections` are kind words to the admin at the
  keys; the rest are plain or quirky facts about Josh, who made the game
  (commits called "ugh", he says "proposed" when unsure, he changed his
  mind on the starting triangle three times), taken from the logs and git
  history, never praise. Josh's own `imsdow.cfg` is unchanged.
- **Why:** the user wanted other admins to feel appreciated, and to learn
  a little about the game's creator without it reading as bragging.
