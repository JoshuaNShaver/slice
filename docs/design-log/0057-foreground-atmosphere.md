# 0057: Foreground dust, light and fog

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted (details Proposed)
- **Decision:** Every region gets a light foreground layer of dust specks,
  light or fog, in front of the world, kept subtle.
- **Proposed details:** it moves faster than the camera (true foreground
  parallax), is drawn on each client only and never synced, sits under
  the HUD, and can be turned off in settings. Per region: the Center,
  paper dust in slanting light; the upper east, almost clean; the priestly
  hill, light shafts and incense; the underworld, slow ash or spores
  with low fog in pits.
