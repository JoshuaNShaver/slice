# 0171: Irregulars are "destroyed"; brides are NPCs

- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Answers to log 0168's questions. The birth cutscene follows
  the book: the newborn is examined by the Sanitary and Social Board
  (Part I §3, §7). An irregular newborn is sentenced to be destroyed, as
  in the book. The Undercroft is a secret: some "destroyed" irregulars are
  recovered there, and the player wakes there, greeted by a new Undercroft
  NPC (name and lines to come). Women are NPCs only (brides in the wedding
  cutscene); players can't be women.
