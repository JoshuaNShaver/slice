# 0201: Sailing between islands

- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Status:** Accepted (not built)
- **Decision:** Islands lie apart across the water, and the water has
  waves, mainly to make boats harder to steer. A boat is a half-disc hull
  with one triangular sail. The use key mirrors it to face the other way.
  Rolling into the sail tips the boat forward, which drives it ahead;
  without that pressure it slows and rights itself. Waves and leaning can
  flip it. A player who falls in sees the screen go black and wakes on the
  shore, losing the gear they carried (the bag's contents, logs included).
- **Why:** the waves and the losses make the trip home with a full bag
  the tense part.
