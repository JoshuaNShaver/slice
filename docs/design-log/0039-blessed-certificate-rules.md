# 0039: Blessed certificate rules

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Decision:** The blessed certificate drops alongside the boss's normal
  certificate, at 1 in 20 boosted by luck. A triangle short of equilateral
  uses it first, and the heir is born equilateral with no irregular roll.
  This also works for an irregular triangle. Anyone else keeps it and
  marries with an ordinary certificate; with only a blessed one, it works
  as an ordinary certificate.
