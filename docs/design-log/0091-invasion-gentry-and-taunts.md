# 0091: Invasion has gentry, not a Captain; moves are taunted

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted (gentry instead of the Captain, taunts); Proposed (numbers, the lines)
- **Decision:**
  - Invasion no longer has a 1-in-5 Captain. Instead 1–3 of the Revolt's
    painted gentry (Gentlemen, pentagons; Nobles, hexagons) fight in its
    camp, at 2× health and damage, all using the Revolt's moves (log 0089).
    They aren't bosses, so invasion no longer discharges.
  - Every Revolt move is telegraphed: the fighter shouts an insult in a
    speech bubble, and makes the move 0.8 s later.
- **Why:** a big-shape Captain in the field read oddly; gentry fit, and a
  taunt gives you a moment to react.
