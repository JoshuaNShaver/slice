# 0139: The terminal companion as line art

- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Status:** Superseded by 0141
- **Decision:** As 0138, but the companion is a large bust portrait in
  Japanese "AA" line-art style (strokes as / \ | _ -, //// hair shading,
  blush, sailor collar), generated by `tools/companion_art.py` into
  `scripts/ui/companion_frames.gd`: rest, blink, and two wave frames with a
  raised hand and happy closed eyes. Drawn at 10 px wide, 8 px narrow.
- **Why:** the user wanted her closer to a detailed AA reference; drawing
  curves and converting them keeps her editable.
