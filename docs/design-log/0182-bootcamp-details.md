# 0182: Bootcamp details

- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Status:** Proposed (first point superseded by 0183; 300 health by 0193)
- **Decision:** Details of log 0181 as built:
  - Quest progress is saved with the family, so heirs carry it on and a
    family does bootcamp once. It starts at any regular birth (a new
    family, or a foundling heir sent up), never for irregulars.
  - Darren's bout: only you fight him and only you can hurt him; others
    see him. His second landed hit loses the bout (like sparring, he can't
    take your last point of health). 300 health, spike up only, with his
    own shouts. Either way you're healed after.
  - The drill yard's sparring partners are called Recruits.
  - The quest you're on shows top right under your pay: its name, the
    objective, and each goal (with counts, ticked when done). An NPC a
    quest sends you to shows a "!" and "E: report".
  - NPC lines that arrive during a cutscene wait until it ends.
