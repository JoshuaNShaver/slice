# 0242: Throwing tips: shots from the center that seek the nearest enemy

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Status:** Accepted (center, seeking, left click / phone button, configurable); Proposed (numbers)
- **Decision:** Throwing tips go in the Points slot. Wearing them, a left
  click (on touch, a Throw button) throws one small spinning metal
  triangle from your shape's center that seeks the nearest hostile
  enemy; with none in range it flies the way you're rolling. Shots are
  data: each piece of gear sets a count and a spread, so later gear can
  throw n shots in different fans.
  - *Proposed:* each shot deals about 40% of your melee corner damage,
    with the normal crit chance and no speed crits; free, 0.4 s between
    throws; stops on terrain and houses; never hurts players; gone after
    about 1.5 s.
- **Why:** with no cursor needed, it works the same with a mouse or a
  phone.
