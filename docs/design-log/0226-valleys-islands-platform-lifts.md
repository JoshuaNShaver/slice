# 0226: Roads curve under the islands; lifts are platforms

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted (shapes *proposed*); valley shape superseded by 0231
- **Decision:** No gangways. The road curves: it dips into a broad valley
  under each raised house's island (the user's first sketch), so the way
  on runs round beneath it (the Center, the barracks, the Noble Quarter).
  Islands are organic: flat where the house stands, curling down at the
  ends, a lumpy belly with a drip or two, each its own shape. Lifts are a
  simple platform: no cage, no shaft, no rails; a plate flush in the ground
  at each stop and a solid platform under the rider. Getting up onto an
  island is left to the movement rework.
- **Why:** the user's call: the ramps looked awful, and the lifts still had
  parts you passed through. Changes log 0222's gangways.
