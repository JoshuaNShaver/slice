# 0211: The Blacksmith's upgrades, and rare ore

- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Status:** Accepted (scaling, visuals and ore numbers Proposed)
- **Decision:** A Blacksmith up the Center's east hill, past Merchants'
  Row, upgrades any piece of gear up to +5: +1 100, +2 200, +3 300 coins;
  +4 500 coins and 10 rare ore; +5 1,000 coins and 25 rare ore. Rare ore
  comes from the next island west, the Quarry Isle, and the dock also
  sells it, dearer than logs.
  - *Proposed scaling:* each level adds a share of the piece's own bonus:
    +10 / 20 / 30 / 50 / 80%. A full set's bonus grows 10% per level of
    its lowest piece (+50% at +5).
  - *Proposed visuals:* the full-set halo is faint at +0 and brightens to
    +4; at +5 it becomes a gilded circle, breathing, with motes circling.
    Icons show "+n".
  - *Proposed ore:* rocks of 8,000 health drop an ore per 2,000 (4 a
    rock), regrow in 5 minutes. The Dock Tradesman pays 600 chits; the
    Harbourmaster sells it from its own stock for 620-1,260 chits or
    45-150 coins (780 / 85 normal). A ferryman on the Logging Isle's
    west shore and the Quarryman launch boats.
  - *Proposed:* the Blacksmith keeps the ore you bring on account, since
    a bag holds only 15.
- **Why:** gives coins a use (§9, Q18) and a long goal past full sets.
