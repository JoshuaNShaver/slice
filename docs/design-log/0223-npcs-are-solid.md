# 0223: NPCs are solid

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted (details *proposed*)
- **Decision:** NPCs have bodies: you bump into them, and they stand in
  your way. *Proposed (built):* they're immovable terrain, so you can also
  stand on them; the elevator guards are solid too. With today's movement
  every shape gets over a square, triangle or pentagon NPC by rolling into
  it and jumping, but nobody clears the Priest (a circle, 88 px), so he
  preaches from a dais over the plaza: you pass under him and marry from
  below. Bigger NPCs stand where they don't close the only road, until the
  movement rework lets shapes clear them.
