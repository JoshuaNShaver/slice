# 0130: Touch buttons: a right-edge column, Home by the stick

- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** On touch, Drink, Chat, Bag and Menu stack in a column on
  the right edge, rising from just above the Jump arc (Drink nearest the
  thumb, Menu highest). Home sits on the left above the stick. Use and
  Take sit just outside the ability arc, closer to the right thumb.
- **Why:** the most-pressed button should be nearest the thumb, and
  buttons that open panels can be a reach away.
