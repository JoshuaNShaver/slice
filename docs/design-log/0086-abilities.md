# 0086: Abilities: rush, nose dive, spike up

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted (the moves and 50 energy); Proposed (prices, keys, numbers, kept until marriage); nose dive jump speed superseded by 0090
- **Decision:** Abilities are bought once from the Quartermaster for pay
  and cost 50 energy per use.
  - **Rush** (Shift, 40 pay): sideways speed and spin ×5.
  - **Nose dive** (W / Up, 60 pay): a jump at 2× jump speed. Coming
    down, the sharpest corner turns to point down and gravity pulls 2×.
  - **Spike up** (S / Down, 80 pay): flip so a face is flat on the
    ground with the sharpest corner up, held for 1 s. Hostile enemies
    within 260 px are thrown up (stunned) to land on it.
  An heir starts with none, like levels and gear.
