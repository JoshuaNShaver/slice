# 0089: Recon's Captain is a huddle; Revolt bosses use moves

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted (the shape of it); Proposed (numbers, names, bosses resist spike up, the guards break when he falls)
- **Decision:**
  - Recon's 1-in-10 Captain (half strength) comes with twice the rebels
    (6–10), a Painted Gentleman (pentagon) and a Painted Noble (hexagon).
    Nobody chases: guards hold spots around him and fight only within
    220 px of them; he keeps within 450 px of his post. When he falls,
    the huddle breaks and they chase. Pay ×5 and the discharge (log 0067).
  - The 1-in-5 find (log 0088) is the Painted Noble, pay ×2. The two no
    longer stack: the Captain's event replaces it.
  - When the soldier spots the Captain, a popup offers **Report back** for
    20 s: back to the barracks at once with double pay (50).
  - Revolt bosses (Captains, Chromatistes) use the players' moves every
    3–5 s: spike up when you're close (throwing you onto them), nose
    dive onto you from middle distance, rush from further out. Their
    corner hits do double damage during a move. Bosses can't be thrown by
    a player's spike up.
- **Why:** a level 5–10 squad should think twice, and a solo level 20 should
  struggle even with a half-strength Captain.
