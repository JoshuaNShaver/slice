# 0064: Twenty chest slots, opened at your house door

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Decision:** Your house holds 20 chest slots. Press interact at your
  house's door to open them, then drag items between bag and chests.
  Every player uses the same door but sees only their own chests. Chests
  are saved on the player's machine, apart from the families (log 0042),
  and checked by the server on joining, like progress (log 0038).
