# 0221: Nothing on the plane is scenery

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted (the per-prop list *proposed*)
- **Decision:** As in the book, everything in the world is in the one
  plane and solid: no object stands "behind" the shapes for them to pass in
  front of. Houses and NPCs are in your way (logs 0222, 0223). Background
  props go: the barracks gate's posts and lintel, the Watch's gate, sign
  posts, the drill-yard fence, the General's tent, crates, flags and
  banners, the forge's chimney, the strongroom door, the ore cart, the log
  pile, Merchants' Row's stalls, the temple's columns. *Proposed:* each is
  removed or rebuilt as solid terrain where it matters (a quay instead of a
  pier on posts, a counter you stand at); place names stay as handwriting
  in the margin, without posts.
- **Exceptions:** what a 3-D onlooker would see is kept as art: water
  drawn in front of a boat's hull (in the plane it would deform under it),
  armor's colour and hatching, fog, the ink pass, paper and stains.
- **Why:** closer to the book; changes logs 0053, 0188's "still scenery".
