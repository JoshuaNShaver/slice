# 0088: Recon's secret stakes

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted (chances, pay and the hint); partly superseded by 0089 (the find is a Noble, the two no longer stack)
- **Decision:** Recon secretly rolls two chances, never shown in the orders:
  - 1 in 5: **a Painted Gentleman** (a painted hexagon of the Revolt's
    gentry, not a boss) stands among the camp's banners. Pay ×2.
  - 1 in 10: **a Captain of the Revolt at half strength** (half health,
    damage and armor). Pay ×5, and bringing him down is the usual
    discharge with a marriage certificate (log 0067).
  Both can happen (×10). Luck raises both, as with invasion's boss. A few
  seconds after landing, the squad's soldier hints in a speech bubble that
  the stakes are higher, without naming numbers.
