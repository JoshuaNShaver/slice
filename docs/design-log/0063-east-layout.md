# 0063: The Noble Quarter and the Priests' Hill

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Decision:** The elevator (log 0054) rises from the Center's east end to
  the Noble Quarter, a plateau of pentagon houses. Your house is there;
  its door opens your chests. A pentagon physician heals regulars in the
  quarter. East of it, the Priests' Hill climbs in terraces of about 15°
  (gentle enough for decagons to stand on) to the Temple of the Circle,
  where the High Priest marries and heals. The temple's sanctum (circles
  only) is sealed for now.
