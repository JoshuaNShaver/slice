# 0205: Merchants trade between two markets

- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Status:** Accepted (not built; numbers TBD)
- **Decision:** A merchant (55°+ apex, log 0177) is the middleman between
  the dock and the Center:
  - **The dock** sells resources for chits or coins, from a limited stock.
    A merchant may post a **work order** there (costs coins) for the rest,
    at a premium, or go gather it themselves.
  - **The Center:** each merchant has a booth that receives orders for
    materials, the price shown. Bringing goods from the dock fills them
    for coins, at a markup.
  - Both markets move: sometimes the dock is oversupplied and cheap;
    sometimes NPCs offer to buy below the dock's price, so stock bought
    cheap can be held. Timing the two is resource management and
    investment.
