# 0230: Speed crits look like a comet

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** At critical speed (log 0229) you look like a comet instead
  of rippling all round: your leading edge glows gold under an ink line,
  brightest dead ahead, and a gold tail tapers out behind (75 px, growing
  with speed to 150), with three flickering ink streaks in it. Nothing
  shows on your back or flanks besides the tail.
- **Why:** reads as speed and direction at a glance; rings all round
  didn't say which way you were going.
