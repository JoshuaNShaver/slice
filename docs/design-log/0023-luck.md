# 0023: Luck scales chance rolls

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Status:** Accepted (birth roll range: proposed)
- **Decision:** Luck multiplies good-outcome chances (drops, marriage
  certificates and similar) by 1 + luck/50, so 50 luck doubles them, capped
  at 100%. Luck is rolled once at birth (0–10*) and doesn't level.
  Irregulars are born with 2x luck.
- **Note:** luck doesn't raise bad-outcome chances such as the irregular-heir
  roll.
