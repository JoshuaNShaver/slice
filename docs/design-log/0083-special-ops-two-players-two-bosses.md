# 0083: Special ops take two players and have two bosses

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Special ops (assassination) take a squad of up to 2,
  mustered like the others. There are always two bosses: Chromatistes,
  and a Captain of the Revolt guarding him in front. Both must fall to
  win; either one brings the campaign's discharge.
