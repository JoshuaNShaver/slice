# 0079: Soldiers catch up; invasion soldiers seek

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** The further a soldier falls behind you, the harder it
  rolls to catch up (top spin rising to faster than a player's full
  roll). Once it engages a foe, it just fights. On invasion, soldiers
  don't orbit or follow: they hunt the Revolt anywhere on the field, and
  only follow you once none are left.
- **Why:** recon soldiers were getting left behind; an invasion army
  should press the attack.
