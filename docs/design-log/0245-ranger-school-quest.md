# 0245: Ranger school, the second quest

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Status:** Accepted (the exam's ending changed by 0251)
- **Decision:** A 50°+ triangle reaching level 20 (bootcamp done) gets a
  quest from the Drill Sergeant: you've been selected for ranger school,
  a special operations unit. You're blindfolded and taken to a new area
  for an entrance exam, a platforming maze, instanced per player. A
  Heroldt descendant is already waiting at the end when you finish. Then
  training like bootcamp, with ranged gear: you're given throwing tips,
  then dummies, recruits, and a bout against the Heroldt descendant. Win
  or lose, the quest ends. After it, the General offers ranger missions
  (log 0248), and the Drill Sergeant can send you to the secret island
  (log 0246).
  - *Proposed (as built):* the Heroldt is **Edric Heroldt** (55°, Scout
    gear). The exam is a staircase of ledges over a pit (fall in: back to
    the start) that forks twice: rolling on drops you into a walled
    pocket; the way on is the higher jump. Training is on the rangers'
    island, from step 3 on (the island admits you and you may wear ranger
    gear from then). Ranger recruits (600 health) keep their distance and
    throw every 2-3 s, pulling their punches. Edric throws faster, has 2,400
    health, and the bout is lost on his fourth landed hit, not the second.
    A save loaded mid-exam puts you back at the start of a fresh course;
    mid-training, the Drill Sergeant sends you back to the island.
