# 0008: Lineage through marriage certificates

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Status:** Accepted (reset rule superseded by 0015)
- **Decision:** A life's goal is to produce an heir. Special events (area
  bosses, dungeons, mazes, others) have a chance to drop a marriage
  certificate. Redeeming it gives an heir with +1 side. Dying without an heir
  resets the lineage to a triangle.
