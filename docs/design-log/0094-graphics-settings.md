# 0094: Graphics settings: framerate cap and Low quality

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** The in-game menu has a max framerate (30, 60, 120, 144 or
  no cap) and Graphics High / Low, saved per machine in
  `user://graphics.cfg`. Low hides the foreground atmosphere, draws the
  background without its ink stains, and renders at 1280x720, scaled up.
- **Why:** older machines (an Intel MacBook) struggled.
