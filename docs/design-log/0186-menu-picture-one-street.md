- **Date:** 2026-10-21

- **Decision:** The menu picture drops the far hills, their houses, the
  temple and the doorway: Flatland has no "behind". Two big lawful
  houses stand on the one ground line with your heir between them.
  Neighbours (a square, a hexagon) come out of their houses' east doors,

  a pentagon strolls between your heir and the right house (its west
  door is the women's). Each keeps to his own stretch, so no one passes
  anyone. The fog, behind and in front, keeps its mouse parallax. The
  rest of log 0185 (sidebar, banner, family panel, hand-drawn look, IM
  Fell English) is accepted.
