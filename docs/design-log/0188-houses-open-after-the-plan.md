# 0188: Every house is drawn open, after the book's plan

- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Status:** Accepted; houses as scenery, and the room layout, superseded by 0222
- **Decision:** Every lawful house, in the game and on the menu, is drawn
  open to show its rooms, unlabelled, after the book's plan of the
  narrator's house with level floors for the side view: the Hall behind
  the men's (west) door, the Sons' cells climbing under the west roof, a
  wall down from the peak, the Bedroom under the east roof, the
  Grandsons and the Wife's apartment on a middle floor, the Cellar and
  the Servants' room on the ground behind the women's (east) door. No
  study (and so no page). Both doors run from partway down their walls to
  the ground, big enough to roll through. Houses are still scenery in the
  game: shapes pass in front, and their rooms are empty (residents would
  overlap passing players). On the menu the right house's family is
  home: the wife (a line) in the Hall, four sons, two grandsons, a
  daughter in the Wife's apartment, and three servants (scullion,
  footman, butler) as sharp triangles. Changes log 0187's rooms.
- **Why:** closer to the book's plan, and doors you could roll through.
