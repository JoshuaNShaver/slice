# 0249: Flat eyes see only what's solid, and the sea

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** A Flatlander's eye (logs 0238-0240) sees only collidable
  things and the water: its picture is drawn from the physics shapes near
  it (FlatMap), not from the world's drawing, so lettering, scenery,
  outlines and hatching never show. Ground, walls, houses, lifts and
  boats are ink (chalk underground); people (players, NPCs, guards,
  enemies) are white, or their paint when coloured (a Revolt's red); the
  sea is blue. All lighten with distance as before.
  Also fixed: the Undercroft hatch swung open, with a platform drawn
  under you, while you merely stood on it; now only for someone dropped
  below it or riding up through it.
- **Why:** NPCs read as ground in ink, and signs and scenery made stripes;
  what a Flatlander could see is what they could bump into.
