# 0051: The caves move to the underworld

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** The training cave (log 0027) belongs to the underworld,
  reached from the Center's south hatch instead of its west wall. Its
  three rooms and the Pretender stay as they are; the underworld will grow
  beyond them later.
- **Why:** irregulars live below, so the bandits, brutes and zealots
  (all irregular) belong there too.
