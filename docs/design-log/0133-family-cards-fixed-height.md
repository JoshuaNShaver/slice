# 0133: Family cards are a fixed height; details scroll

- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Each family card on the main menu has a fixed height: its
  title on top, its buttons (Play and Delete, or Found family) pinned to
  the bottom, and everything between scrolls when it runs long.
- **Why:** long details pushed Play below a phone's screen.
