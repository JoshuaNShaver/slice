# 0184: Darren's line, always a step ahead

- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Status:** Accepted (not built past bootcamp)
- **Decision:** Darren Heroldt is the player's rival and friend across
  the whole game, like Red in Pokémon: always ahead of you. He is your
  friend in the first mission. He is kidnapped by the Colour Revolt. His
  line rises in rank, and since your families may die between quests,
  later you mostly deal with his descendants. The Heroldts reach every
  secret before you: they meet the Architect first, and find the Third
  Dimension first. The Heroldts are their own family, apart from yours,
  and remember your family only as what it is now.
