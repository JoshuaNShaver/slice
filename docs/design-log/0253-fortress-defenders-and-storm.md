# 0253: The fortress's defenders, and a rougher storm

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Status:** Accepted (the features; keep still in the storm); Proposed (numbers)
- **Decision:** Building on log 0250.
  - **The storm is rougher, and keeping still is how you ride it** (the
    user's choice): waves ×2.6, up from ×1.7. A passenger who stays put
    stays aboard. Rolling about, such as dodging arrows, can throw you
    over. The Boatman already says "Keep low and keep still." The raid's
    sea holds water only between its shores, so storm waves never flood
    the courtyard, and they're never drawn above a pier.
  - **An enemy boat** is moored at the fortress pier with 2 lookouts and
    3 archers aboard. Your rowboat lands just behind it.
  - **Painted archers** (55° triangles) lob a fan of 5 arcing shots that
    home in but turn slower than a player can dodge, and now and then
    stand still, glowing, to charge one hard straight shot. They keep to
    their boat.
  - **Lookouts** (small equilateral triangles) run for the **back door**
    at the foot of the crags once you're within 700 px. The door hangs
    from the ceiling of a tunnel under the crags: it swings up for a
    fleeing lookout and drops behind it. Kill them first and only the 3
    skirmishers hold the wall.
  - **The alarm**, if a lookout gets in: 10 archers come down the crags
    from the wall top, one every 0.6 s, and 5 pentagons (rush) and 2
    hexagons (nose dive) guard the winch.
  - **The Castellan** is in his courtyard from the start and goes after
    anyone inside the fortress, however far. He uses his moves about
    twice as often (every 1.4–2.4 s), and throws volleys and charged shots
    as he closes in.
  - **The Flatlander view** shows the raid's sea too.
- **Why:** the raid needed something to do on the way across and a reason
  to hurry at the landing. Tests showed a triangle sitting flat on the
  deck is the steadiest thing aboard. Make the deck slick enough to throw
  a still passenger, and a moving one goes too.
