# 0077: Barracks build details

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Proposed (built); the boss-kill discharge superseded by 0092, metal prices by 0206
- **Decision:** Details chosen while building the barracks:
  - People: a Drill Sergeant (isosceles), the Quartermaster (equilateral,
    a tradesman), the Surgeon (square, heals on request) and the General
    (a regular pentagon).
  - Squads muster at the General for 10 s; recon takes up to 4, invasion
    up to 8. Anyone may go; ranks above triangle too (log 0073).
  - Pay on success: recon 25, invasion 80, special ops 200. Leaving the
    field early (home key or otherwise) forfeits it. Pay is kept by heirs.
  - A boss kill discharges every squad member still on the field with a
    certificate. Boss odds use the squad's luckiest member.
  - Military tiers: leather / bronze / iron / steel give +2 / 4 / 7 / 10
    (plating +3 / 6 / 10 / 15), so steel equals lilac. Bronze 30, iron
    90, steel 240 pay per piece. No military pigment, so no metal sets.
  - The Revolt's paint is vermilion. Soldiers wear bronze; players pass
    through them and can't hurt them. Bosses: a Captain of the Revolt
    (invasion, fights as if in lilac) and Chromatistes, the Painter
    (special ops, gilded).
  - The underworld lift takes any regular up; only going down needs 5+.
