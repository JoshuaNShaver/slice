# 0222: Houses are big, solid and lived in

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted (sizes and layouts *proposed*); gangways superseded by 0226; the barracks hall by 0232, the dock house by 0233, the underworld houses' 2.3x by 0236
- **Decision:** Houses are solid and much larger, big enough that going
  in makes sense. Since a house blocks the way, each is placed so the road
  goes under it or past it, after the user's two sketches:
  - **Raised:** on a floating mound with the road running beneath; a
    floating gangway climbs to it (about 26°: up to hexagons, by 180°/n).
    In the plane, anything you pass under has to float, as the Center's
    ledges do.
  - **Sunken:** on the road under an overhang, at the road's end. Men go
    in by the west door; the east door opens on the women's side, so for
    men the house is a dead end and the way on is the upper road (here,
    the lift beside it).
- *Proposed (built):*
  - Walls, floors and roof are terrain (`House`), drawn at their true
    thickness; doors are gaps. The women's door is a slit too low for any
    polygon (a woman is a line).
  - 760 px across (820 in the Noble Quarter), rooms about three shapes
    tall: the Hall with treads up to the middle floor (Grandsons, Wife),
    then the Bedroom and the Sons' cells under the roof. Floors climb in
    steps of about 80 px, so upper rooms wait on the movement rework for
    triangles. Changes log 0188's room layout.
  - The Center: one raised house over the low road west, one sunken under
    the Noble Quarter's shelf (extended west over it), the lift up just
    west of it. The barracks hall and the dock house are raised; the
    Quarter's three houses (yours one of them) are raised over its street.
    Underworld houses (crooked, 2.3× bigger) have a door at each foot: you
    roll right through them.
  - Residents are back: the Workman in the raised house, the Shopkeeper
    and the Strongroom Keeper (its cellar is the strongroom) in the sunken.
- **Why:** a solid world needs houses you can get past; changes 0188.
