# 0072: A drill yard for levels 1–5, dropping leather

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted; straw dummies superseded by 0224
- **Decision:** The barracks has a drill yard with straw dummies and
  sparring partners. New soldiers spar there until about level 3–5. It
  drops leather gear, the tier below bronze. Mission levels are a guide:
  you may take a mission under-leveled.
