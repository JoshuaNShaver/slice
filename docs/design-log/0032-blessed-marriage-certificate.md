# 0032: Blessed marriage certificate

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** The cave boss has a 1-in-20 chance to drop a rare blessed
  certificate. Redeemed at a priest by a triangle, the heir is born
  equilateral at once, skipping the apex steps (log 0031).
- **Open:** whether it replaces the boss's normal certificate or drops
  alongside it (assumed alongside), and what it does for shapes that are
  already regular.
