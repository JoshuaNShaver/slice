# 0229: Every corner hits, and speed crits

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted (numbers Proposed; the sound-wave look superseded by 0230)
- **Decision:** The 0.3 s hit cooldown is per corner, not per target: when
  several of your corners strike an enemy, each one deals its damage. At
  **critical speed** (650+ px/s, rising not counted) every corner hit is a
  crit (double damage, gold "!" number), and for 0.15 s after slowing, so
  every corner of one impact counts. Luck crits don't stack on top. While
  at critical speed a sound wave ripples round your edge: rings of your
  outline spreading out, gold under ink, bunched on the side you're heading.
  Enemies don't speed-crit. Answers open question 14 (log 0047).
- **Why:** many-sided shapes only ever landed their first corner. Speed
  crits reward movement: rolling and jumping stay under 650 (top ~630 at max
  torque), rush always gets there, a long nose dive often does.
