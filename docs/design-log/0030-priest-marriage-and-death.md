# 0030: The priest marries you; death ends the family

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** A priest (a circle) stands on the Center plaza. Press E near
  them with a certificate to marry. The certificate is used up and you
  continue as the heir, who starts fresh at level 1 with no gear. Heirs with
  4+ sides gain a side. Only equilateral triangles have square heirs (log
  0009); other triangles' heirs stay triangles of the same kind. Any heir
  has the 1-in-10 irregular roll. Death is permanent: since the heir takes
  over at once, you never hold one in reserve, so dying always ends the
  family (log 0015).
- **Answers** the old open question 5 (what an heir inherits): nothing but
  the family.
- **Note:** no starting triangle is equilateral (log 0016), so for now no
  family can reach square. Open question 2 decides how one can.
