# 0084: Provisions: food that heals, bought for pay

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted (items, numbers and seller Proposed)
- **Decision:** The Quartermaster sells provisions for combatant pay:
  hardtack (20% of max health, 3 pay), cheese (35%, 6 pay) and salt pork
  (50%, 10 pay). Unlike a potion, a meal heals over 8 s; eating more adds
  to what's left, and it stops when you're full. Eat by double-click or a
  quick-use key. Provisions survive marriage, like potions.
- **Why:** potions only drop in the cave; pay needed a cheap, repeatable
  way to heal. Healing over time keeps potions the emergency option.
