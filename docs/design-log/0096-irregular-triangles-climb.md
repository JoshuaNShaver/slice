# 0096: Irregular triangles climb too; equilaterals always have a square

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** An irregular triangle's heir takes the usual 1-in-10
  irregular roll instead of always staying irregular. Irregular triangles
  carry an apex like isosceles ones (45°, +5° a generation, up to 60°), and
  the heir's apex widens whether or not it rolls irregular; at 60° a
  regular roll makes it equilateral. An equilateral's heir is always a
  regular square: the only heir with no irregular roll. Older irregular
  triangle saves start at 45°.
- **Why:** irregular triangles bred true, so one bad roll trapped a family
  for good (unless a 1-in-20 blessed certificate turned up).
