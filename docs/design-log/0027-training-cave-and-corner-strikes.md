# 0027: Training cave west of the Center, and corner strikes

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Status:** Accepted (layout and numbers Proposed)
- **Decision:** A small training cave opens in the Center's west wall. Its
  three rooms have different chalk colors and get harder going in: the Rose
  Hollow (bandits, levels 1–7), the Mint Gallery (brutes, 7–14) and the Lilac
  Deep (zealots, 14–20). The boss, the Pretender, waits at the back of the
  lilac room and is balanced for level 20. Enemies are irregular polygons
  (the book's criminals). They chase players inside their room only and
  respawn after 12 s (the boss after 90 s).
  *Proposed:* combat works by contact. One of your corners striking an
  enemy at 70+ px/s deals your corner damage, and enemy corners hurt you the
  same way. Faces do nothing. This works for rolling into enemies as well as
  landing on them.
- **Why:** a place to train and earn gear before real dungeons exist.
