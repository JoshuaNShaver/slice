# 0092: A boss win earns a chance at the certificate

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Bringing down a Revolt boss (recon's Captain, special
  ops) no longer discharges everyone. Each squad member rolls 1 in 5 for
  a discharge with a marriage certificate (luck raises it), and the General
  gives it, like a promotion, once they're back at the barracks. A miss
  still gets the pay and a word from the General. Invasion has no boss,
  so no certificate: it's for levels and pay.
- **Why:** a certificate should feel earned, not farmed.
