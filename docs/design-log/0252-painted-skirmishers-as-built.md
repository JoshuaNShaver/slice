# 0252: Painted skirmishers, as built

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Decision:** Building log 0247. A painted skirmisher is a 50°
  triangle with 300 health that throws every 2–3 s from up to 600 px and
  backs off inside 300 px. Its tips hurt players and the army's soldiers.
  Mission strength scales its tips and body: recon ×1, invasion ×1.6,
  special ops ×2.5. Skirmishers stand at the back of the field. One held
  in place (recon's huddle) still throws at anyone in range. Any thrower
  now notices you as far away as it can throw. Recon's soldier warns of
  them.
