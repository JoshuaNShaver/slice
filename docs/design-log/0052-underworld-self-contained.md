# 0052: The underworld is self-contained, and snubs regulars

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted (details accepted in 0059)
- **Decision:** The underworld is almost a world of its own, since
  irregulars can't leave it. Regular shapes may go down, but most
  underworld NPCs refuse to deal with them.
- **Proposed details:** it has its own hub with a respawn point, a healer
  and a priest who marries irregulars; C sends irregulars there instead of
  the Center. Refusal is fixed per NPC, not a dice roll each time: most
  refuse regulars outright, and a few (a fence, a smuggler) deal with
  anyone at a markup.
- **Why:** irregulars need a full game below; the snub mirrors how the
  overworld treats them.
