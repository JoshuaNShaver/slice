# 0243: Abilities are equipped into key slots

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Status:** Accepted (equippable); Proposed (slots, mixing)
- **Decision:** You own abilities and equip them. *Proposed:* three slots,
  W, S and Shift, each holding one owned ability made for that key
  (melee: nose dive, spike up, rush; ranger: volley, charged shot, double
  jump), swapped on the character sheet. Melee and ranger abilities mix
  freely.
