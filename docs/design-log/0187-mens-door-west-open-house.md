# 0187: Men's door on the west; the menu shows a house's rooms

- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Status:** Accepted; its rooms superseded by 0188
- **Decision:** Houses had their doors backwards. As in the book ("on the
  East is a small door for the Women; on the West a much larger one for
  the Men"), the men's large door is on the west wall and the women's
  small door on the east. Your house's chest door moves with it, to the
  west side. The menu's right house is cut open like the book's plan of
  the narrator's house: the Hall behind the men's door, Sons, My Study and
  Bedroom under the roof, Grandsons over the Cellar, Servants over the
  Women's room by the women's door, with level floors. His wife (a line)
  stands in the Hall, a son (pentagon), grandson (hexagon), servant (a
  sharp triangle) and daughter (a line) in their rooms; the master, a
  square, goes out of the men's door toward your heir and back into the
  Hall. Changes log 0060's door sides and log 0186's walkers.
- **Why:** the book's law, and the plan is a nice thing to see.
