# 0212: Promissory notes, and the family strongroom

- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Status:** Accepted (place, sizes and later prices Proposed)
- **Decision:**
  - **Promissory notes:** the Harbourmaster sells notes for coins. They
    stack, and a booth order on Merchants' Row takes them in place of
    logs. *Proposed:* one note per log at the dock's coin price, taken
    from the log stock; up to 50 in one bag slot; an order takes logs
    first, then notes; drowning loses them like the rest of the bag.
  - **The family strongroom:** a family buys its first chest for 1,000
    coins: storage that every heir of that family keeps. *Proposed:* the
    Strongroom Keeper on the Center plaza's west edge; 10 slots a chest;
    a second for 3,000 and a third for 8,000; saved with the family, so
    it's lost when the family ends. Anyone regular may use it.
- **Why:** notes let merchants buy ahead and fill big orders without
  hauling wood; the strongroom is safe keeping that outlives an heir.
  This lets a triangle family pass gear to its heirs, which changes log
  0041's "triangle runs always start with nothing".
