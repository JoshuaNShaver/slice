# 0042: Chests belong to the player, not the family

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** House chests are kept by the player across families. When a
  family ends, the chests stay; a new family (a triangle) can't use them
  until it reaches a square again (log 0041).
- **Why:** past runs' gear rewards climbing back to a square, rather than
  vanishing with each family.
