# 0036: Five gear slots from the book, and a bag

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Status:** Accepted (stat numbers Proposed); click-to-wear superseded by 0043
- **Decision:** The book has no equipment, so the slots come from what it
  does have. Each slot gives one stat and never changes your outline:
  **Points** (corner caps, force), **Plating** (armor, rigidity), **Pigment**
  (contraband colour from the Colour Revolt, vigor), **Eye** (a lens for
  sight recognition, luck) and **Voice** (a pipe for your cry, mettle; it
  becomes an ability slot once abilities exist). Cave enemies drop a random
  slot at their room's tier.
  Items go into a 10-slot bag. Nothing is taken by touch: the **pickup key
  (G)** grabs the nearest of your items. In the bag, click to wear an item
  and right-click to drop it. On the character sheet, click a slot to take
  it off. Dropped items stay yours until they vanish (90 s).
  *Proposed:* tiers 1–4 give +3 / 6 / 10 / 15, and plating +4 / 9 / 15 / 22.
- **Replaces** the auto-equip rule in 0029.
