# 0227: The underworld lift runs down a shaft

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** The Center's lift down to the Undercroft runs through a real
  shaft, 120 px wide, cut out of the Center's ground, the earth band and the
  Undercroft's roof. The rider no longer passes through ground. A solid hatch
  of two leaves caps the top stop so nobody falls in past the guards; it
  hangs open only while the platform passes. The noble lift, through open
  air, is unchanged.
- **Why:** the user's call: shapes can't pass through the ground. Changes
  log 0226's "no shaft" for this lift.
