# 0070: The general's three mission tiers

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Proposed (built)
- **Decision:** The general at the barracks gives instanced missions:
  - **Recon** (levels 5–10): 3–5 enemies, 1 friendly soldier.
  - **Invasion** (levels 10–20): 20+ enemies, 10 friendlies, 1 in 5 has a
    Revolt boss.
  - **Special ops** (assassination, level 20): solo, 5 enemies and a boss.
  Defeating a boss completes the campaign (log 0067).
