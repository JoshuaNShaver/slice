# 0241: Rangers, a ranged build

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Rangers fight at range: their gear throws small spinning
  metal triangles. They deal less damage than melee in exchange for
  distance. Ranger school is a quest (log 0245), and quests are the
  account's (log 0183), so it is done once. A 50°+ triangle (isosceles
  50°/55°, irregular 50°+, equilateral) at level 20 starts it. Once it's
  passed, any of the account's shapes, squares and up included, may wear
  ranger gear. It's a build, not a class: ranger armor follows the melee
  Quartermaster's stat curve with different profiles, and a ranger may mix
  in melee gear.
