# 0239: Flat eyes see ink on paper, 120° wide, and hold still

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted (all-ink colouring and range superseded by 0240)
- **Decision:** In the Flatlander's view (log 0238), near things are solid
  ink fading into the paper with distance, and empty air is paper
  (underground: chalk fading into slate). No fill colours or edge lines.
  Each eye sees 120° (`\view fov <degrees>`, 20-180): the two eyes centred
  on west and east, the single eye on where it faces. The single eye
  turns only when the mouse moves (its direction from the screen's
  middle), so jumping moves the world in the bar instead of the eye.
- **Why:** black sky and brown ground read backwards in an ink-on-paper
  world, and fill colours and hatching made stripes; 180° stretched the
  view; aiming from your shape made the eye swing when you jumped.
