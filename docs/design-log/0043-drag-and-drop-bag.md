# 0043: Drag and drop in the bag; double-click to use

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Bag items are dragged and dropped to move them between
  slots, including into the 1–5 quick-use slots. **Double-click** an item
  to use it: wear gear, drink a potion. Replaces the single-click to wear
  in 0036. *Proposed:* right-click still drops an item, and dragging gear
  onto (or off) a character-sheet slot wears (or removes) it.
- **Why:** lets players arrange the bag and fill the hotbar.
