# 0185: Main menu: a sidebar beside a drawn picture

- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Status:** Accepted; the picture superseded by 0186
- **Decision:** The main menu keeps its parchment but becomes a torn-paper
  sidebar on the left (Offline / Online, Play, version) beside a picture
  filling the rest, with the title on a torn banner. Play opens a
  "Select a Family" panel over the picture with the 3 family cards;
  online, the sign-in steps show in that panel, which opens itself when
  one needs you. Shapes are drawn as in the game (no faces).
  *Proposed:* buttons, fields and panels drawn by hand (`SketchBox`:
  doubled pencil lines that overshoot the corners, edge shading); menu
  lettering in IM Fell English (OFL, `assets/fonts/fell*.ttf`); the
  picture is a side-on street (`MenuScene`): far hills with houses and the
  temple, two lawful houses and a doorway, the world's ground hatching,
  soft fog in front and behind, a square and a hexagon tumbling past, and
  your first family's heir standing in the middle with its name. Layers
  shift slightly with the mouse; Low graphics keeps it still, without
  fog. A Quit button on desktop. Esc closes the panel.
- **Why:** the sidebar leaves the middle for art; the old centered column
  had none.
