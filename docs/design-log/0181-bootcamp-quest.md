# 0181: The first quest: bootcamp, and Darren Heroldt

- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** After the birth, the Priest sends you to the Drill
  Sergeant. His rundown of combat is a cutscene (in full the first time):
  corners hurt, hit moving, best from above, and he shows it on a dummy,
  jumping surprisingly high. Then: knock down 5 dummies, report back,
  spar 5 recruits, report back and get leather plating, open the
  character sheet and wear it, report back. He introduces your senior,
  Darren of the family Heroldt (spike up, full leather), the start of a
  long friendship and rivalry. Darren's first hit leaves you at 2 health.
  Win, and he talks of your potential; lose, and he gives constructive
  feedback and says he's glad to be working with you. The quest ends.
