# 0019: The Center hub and rolling physics

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Decision:** The Center is a plaza at ground level. The land sinks west
  toward the war front, rises east (a gentle slope, then a 35° one) toward
  the upper districts, has jump-through ledges climbing north, and has a
  sealed hatch marking the way south. Region exits are walls until the
  regions exist. Shapes are real physics polygons simulated on the server:
  rolling pivots over the leading corner, so slope grip (180°/n) comes from
  the geometry alone. Players pass through each other. In mid-air you can
  drift sideways but not turn.
- **Why:** The hub shows each direction's region at a glance, and the 35°
  slope shows off grip: triangles to pentagons rest on it, hexagons and up
  roll off.
