# 0216: Heirlooms: armor passed down through the strongroom

- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Status:** Accepted (details Proposed)
- **Decision:** A family with a strongroom (log 0212) also gets free
  heirloom slots, kept apart from its chests, for the last generation's
  armor. When an heir takes over, what the old one wore is passed down
  into them. If a slot already holds a piece, the player is shown the old
  and the new and clicks the one to pass down; the other is lost.
  - *Proposed:* one heirloom slot per gear slot (five), each taking only
    its own kind of gear; drag them out to the bag, or back in, at the
    Strongroom. The choice opens once the wedding cutscene ends and can
    be closed and made later at the Strongroom; until then the old piece
    stays. A choice still open at the next succession keeps the old piece.
    Without a strongroom, worn gear is lost as before.
- **Why:** good armor can stay in the family across generations.
