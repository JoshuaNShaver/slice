# 0037: Health regen and healing potions

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Status:** Accepted (numbers Proposed)
- **Decision:** Health regenerates 0.5% of max per second in combat and 2%
  out of combat (5 s without hitting or being hit). Healing potions restore
  40% of max health. They drop from cave enemies (10% each, boosted by luck),
  and the boss always drops one. Drink with F (rebindable) or by clicking one
  in the bag; you can't drink at full health. Potions survive marriage.
