# 0246: The secret island and the ranger quartermaster

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Status:** Accepted (chits for starting gear, coins for the rest, rations, double jump); Proposed (travel, numbers)
- **Decision:** The Drill Sergeant sends a graduate to a secret island
  where the ranger quartermaster sells the starting ranger gear for chits
  and everything else for coins only. Ranger armor follows the melee
  Quartermaster's stat curve. He sells double jump, and **rations**:
  they stack to 30 in one slot and heal less than salt pork.
  - *Proposed (as built):* talking to the Drill Sergeant sends a ranger
    (or one in training) to the island; the Ferryman takes you back to the
    barracks. The island also has a Camp Medic and the Ranger Instructor.
    Ranger tiers: Scout (100 chits a piece; set +10 luck), then for coins
    Tracker 150 (+10 mettle), Outrider 300 (+10 torque), Pathfinder 750
    (+15 luck), Ranger 2,000 (+15 luck, +20 force). Rations heal 15% over
    8 s, 2 coins each.
