# 0247: Rebel rangers in the missions

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Status:** Accepted (rebel rangers in all three missions); Proposed (details)
- **Decision:** The Colour Revolt fields rangers in recon, invasion and
  special ops. *Proposed:* Painted skirmishers keep their distance and
  throw one spinning tip every 2-3 s, which you can dodge. Recon 1-2,
  invasion 4-6, special ops trades two bodyguards for them.
