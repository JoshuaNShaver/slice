# 0044: Quick-use slots are bag slots

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** The five quick-use slots (keys 1–5, rebindable) are extra
  bag slots, so the bag holds 15 items. Dragging an item there moves it;
  its key uses it (drink a potion, wear gear). Loot fills the bag before
  the quick slots, and an heir keeps potions and certificates in the slots
  they were in. Also accepts 0043's proposals: right-click drops an item,
  and gear drags onto or off a character-sheet slot to wear or remove it.
