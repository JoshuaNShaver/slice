# 0134: Playable upright

- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** The game plays held either way. A window taller than it is
  wide gets a 720x1280 minimum view (1280x720 otherwise), so menus and
  text keep their size and upright play sees more above and below, less
  ahead. The installed app turns freely, and nothing asks to turn the
  phone sideways.
- **Why:** turning the phone just to type a name or pick a family felt
  forced. Supersedes the turn-sideways part of 0122 and the 1280x720-only
  minimum of 0126.
