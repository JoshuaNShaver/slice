# 0244: Ranger abilities: volley, charged shot, double jump

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Status:** Accepted (moves); Proposed (energy, numbers)
- **Decision:** Sold by the ranger quartermaster (log 0246).
  - **Volley (W):** throws 3x your tips' shot count in arcs; holding W
    throws them farther. The shots seek the nearest enemy.
  - **Charged shot (S):** one straight shot at the nearest enemy that
    doesn't seek after it leaves, so a moving target can dodge it.
    Holding charges its damage; a glow flashes when the critical hold
    time is reached, and a shot released then crits.
  - **Double jump (Shift slot):** a second jump in mid-air.
  - *Proposed:* volley and charged shot cost 50 energy like other
    abilities, paid on release; double jump is free. Volley: held up to
    1 s; the shots seek from 0.4 s up the arc. Charged shot: full (2x
    damage) at 1.2 s, critical window 1.2-1.5 s, lets go by itself at 4 s;
    a ring closes round you as it charges and flashes gold in the window.
    Prices in coins: volley 600, charged shot 900, double jump 400.
