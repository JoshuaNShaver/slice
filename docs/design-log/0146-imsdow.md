# 0146: imsdow, and what she says

- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** The terminal companion is named **imsdow** (a name tag
  under her). She's found asleep 30% of the time the terminal opens; while
  she sleeps (that, or after 45 s without typing), each command gets a
  mumble ("mmm, a few more minutes..."), and the third wakes her: a waking
  line and her blush. Awake, she says something after 35% of commands and
  every 25-45 s at rest: shape puns, or bits of the game's design history
  with their log numbers. Lines and these numbers live in
  `assets/companion/imsdow.cfg`. A line shows for 6 s or longer if long,
  in a fixed 3-line space so she doesn't move. Picture offsets are
  sideways only: her frames are cut off at the bottom, and moving them up
  or down showed as her bobbing.
- **Why:** the user asked for her name, sleepy mumbles, puns, and lore.
