# 0071: Combatant pay buys bronze, iron and steel

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted; name superseded by 0204, prices by 0206
- **Decision:** Missions pay a currency, combatant pay. Merchants sell
  military gear for it in a bronze → iron → steel ladder, shown as
  crosshatch patterns (log 0069). Military gear is bought, not dropped.
