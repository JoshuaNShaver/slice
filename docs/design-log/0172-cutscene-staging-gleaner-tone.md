# 0172: Cutscene staging, the Gleaner, and tone

- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Answers the rest of log 0168. Cutscenes are a full-screen
  overlay drawn on the client; the world keeps running and the server
  keeps the player safe until it ends. A cutscene plays in full the first
  time; after that a tap or key skips it. The Undercroft NPC is **the
  Gleaner**, an irregular who takes "destroyed" infants from the Board's
  chute; they greet you on waking and point you to the Bonesetter and the
  cave. Tone: the officials are sincerely pompous, never cruel on purpose.
