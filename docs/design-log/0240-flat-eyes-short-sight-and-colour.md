# 0240: Flat eyes see 450 px, and see colour

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Status:** Accepted (how colour is chosen superseded by 0249)
- **Decision:** A Flatlander's eye (logs 0238, 0239) sees 450 px
  (`\view range <px>`, 100-1400): fog lightens things with distance and
  hides them entirely by then, so past it, or with nothing in view, the
  eye sees only the background. Plain things (ground, uncoloured shapes,
  slate) stay ink (chalk underground); coloured things show their colour,
  fading the same way, so a Revolt's paint shows red. The side view's
  guide lines end at the range.
- **Why:** seeing across the whole screen didn't feel like fog, and
  all-ink hid colour, which matters (the Revolt paints itself).
